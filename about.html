About Helping Hands

We believe that everyone can do something good for their community.

A simple idea with a useful purpose

Helping Hands started with a simple thought: people should support each other whenever they can. Our website shares a few causes where volunteers and donors can offer help.

We try to work with local people, understand what is needed and use donations carefully. This is a student website project, so the activities shown here are examples.
